==== 1.1.0 | 2026-10-15 | src 24c26ab2ed4d | gen e66a3d27dbe7 ====
[b]Version 1.1.0 · 15 October 2026[/b]
• Two settings in Options > Add-ons. [b]Offer antiquated tiles for Wonders[/b] (on by default) switches the mod's offer on or off without leaving the game; off, a Wonder's placement screen shows only the game's own sites. [b]Antiquated means[/b] picks how old a building has to be: from any earlier age (the default, the rule so far) or from two ages back, which in the Modern age counts Antiquity buildings only and in the Exploration age leaves only the game's own sites. Both are kept in the shared settings store the bundled Tower Settings Keeper looks after, so they hold across a restart, and your save stays out of it. The mod reads them once and keeps them in memory, so the production list stays as quick as before.
• The mod's text is in German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) as well as English, following the game's language setting.
• Rila Monastery isn't offered an antiquated tile any more. It can't stand next to a district, and nearly every urban tile has one beside it, so the game would turn the site down and you'd see a few seconds of empty hex before the mod put your buildings back.
• The README and the Workshop page now name the Wonders the mod leaves out and why: Ubudiah Mosque has to stand on a wet feature, Shwedagon Zedi Daw and Muzibu Azaala Mpanga beside a lake, and Rila Monastery away from any district. Battersea Power Station, Hale o Keawe and Havana Harbor carry a next-to-land rule the mod doesn't check, so they're left out as well, as they have been since 0.3.0.

==== 1.0.1 | 2026-10-07 | src 768139c8b472 | gen 1ec3dcaf0131 ====
[b]Version 1.0.1 · 7 October 2026[/b]
• Settings kept after a restart. Civilization VII reads back only the first entry in its mod storage, whichever one a mod asks for, so options set in one session could come back as another mod's data or not at all, and a mod saving its options could copy that data under its own name. Build Wonders Over Antiquated Buildings now carries the Tower Settings Keeper file, which keeps every mod's settings inside the one entry the game reads correctly and repairs a store another mod has already put out of order, without deleting anything. Build Wonders Over Antiquated Buildings saves nothing there itself. It carries the file so that the settings of the other mods you play with stay readable, even if you never install the keeper.
• The file runs before any other script and changes nothing else in the mod. If several mods carry it, or the standalone Tower Settings Keeper is installed too, one copy runs and the newest build wins. Nothing to set up: existing settings carry over. Tower Settings Keeper: [url=https://github.com/tmtmiller1/civilizationvii_tower-settings-keeper]GitHub[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3815023570]Steam[/url].

==== 1.0.0 | 2026-09-24 | src 5f96ea809e87 | gen 829528c15db2 ====
[b]Version 1.0.0 · 24 September 2026[/b]

First release. A city that filled its center in Antiquity has nowhere left to put a Wonder. This mod lets you build one over the buildings that age left behind.
• Pick a Wonder in production and urban tiles whose buildings are all from an earlier age are offered on the placement screen alongside the usual ones, with a yield preview.
• The old buildings go and the Wonder’s construction site appears in the same instant. No empty hex is drawn.
• The walls stay. They return on the Wonder’s district as soon as it lands.
• One citizen per building that housed one comes back as pending population, and the game’s own Grow City prompt asks where each settles.
• The mod decides where a Wonder may go, not whether it may be built. A Wonder that is locked, not yet unlocked, or already standing somewhere in the world stays refused exactly as the game refuses it. Every placement rule in the game’s data is checked before a tile is offered.
• If the game turns the cleared site down after all, the buildings and walls are put back within a few seconds and no citizens are displaced.
• No database change. One script. Wonders keep their class, uniqueness, cinematics, terrain rules and AI behavior. A save loads with the mod on or off, and it can join a game in progress.
• Works in multiplayer, including over a LAN session.
• English only for now; the text is two strings, so a translation is a small job. Ask for a language.
